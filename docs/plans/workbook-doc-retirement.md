## What "retired" means, per row

A workbook or document is **retired** when all three hold:

1. Vision is the system of record for every field anyone still uses, meaning a person
   enters it in Vision.
2. The file has not been edited for a full month-end close.
3. The watched source is disabled, and the `workbook_sync` surface is `live` (for the
   daily log).

Rows W2 and W8 meet (1) and (2) today; W8 needs only (3).
