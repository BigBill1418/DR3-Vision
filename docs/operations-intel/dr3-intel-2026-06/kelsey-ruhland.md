## 1. What MRC reporting do you handle? Cadence, format, who you submit to, what gets verified before submission.

Every week I submit a transportation log of material moved from woodland to Stockton, a log of all woodland material that has left Stockton, and an updated trailer list.  I verify the information against the daily logs/MyMRC entries.  I also track our inbound vs outbound Woodland material in Stockton so I can match up Haul/Material numbers on the Stockton outbound log.  All of which will no longer be necessary after Stockton's closure.  (I will share all these files with you so you have them for your records). 

That being said, the trailer list should be a part of DR3 Vision - any team member should be able to check in and see what (if anything) is being stored in the yard and how long it's been there.

Other than that, I don't do any MRC reporting.  I will occasionally inform them of things that may affect our inbound or outbound rates such as driver shortage, processor shortage, terex being down for a significant amount of time, etc.

## 5. What compliance reports do you generate for DR3, and what data do you need for them?

Because we enter our data into MyMRC, MRC has the information they need to form their own compliance reports (recycling rate, inbound/outbound ratio, etc.).  We are required to stay compliant on background admin things (see list below).  These don't require regular reporting, but MRC could require proof of compliance at any point.
COI (general liability, vehicles, workers comp)
yearly bed bug training for each employee (must be documented)
bed bug mitigation plan
bed bug signage in facilities
compliance with local, county, and state permits (Oregon doesn't require much, but Woodland has property assessment, business license registration, business name registry)
annual scale inspections and registration
fire systems inspection
fire sprinkler inspection
fire inspection (MRC also has a fire/safety inspection)
annual desk audits for downstream vendors
Business closure plan (Woodland's plan will need to be updated after Stockton closes)
remediation of contamination in case of closure
Hazardous Waste materials business plan and reporting (this will need to be filed for Woodland AND Stockton for 2026)
