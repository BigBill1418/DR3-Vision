## 1. From the floor — what equipment and processes do you work with? Walk me through a typical shift.

I work with the forklift. Typical shift consist of loading and unloading trucks, clearing the floor, and keeping lines full.

## 2. Equipment downtime — when something breaks (like the Terex), how is that recorded today? What goes into the spreadsheet, what stays word-of-mouth, what gets lost?

We reported to the office. We tell the manager what happened, at what time did it happen, and what we have to do to try to fix the problem.

## 3. Quality issues — when a mattress can't be processed (wet, damaged, illegal contents inside), what happens and how is it recorded?

We write it down on the processor sheet and we need to report it to a manager.

## 4. What slows production down most often? What would you change if you could?

The pocket coils and wood slow us down. If we had a wood chipper it would help a lot. Also making pocket coil bales helps a lot.

## 5. Production volume — is the number recorded each day accurate to what you actually processed? What would make it more accurate?

To my knowledge it is accurate. But if we had a better way to record the beds that are given to the processors, it would be a great help. Sometimes beds are not visible and the count gets messed up.

## 6. What information do you wish supervisors had about the floor that they don't today?

When employees do things that make the process harder. For example not cleaning their area or putting materials where they don’t go.

## 7. Safety — are there safety events or near-misses that should be tracked alongside production?

Yes, sometimes the bales are stacked high and it feels unsafe

## 8. For your role specifically — what would make the work easier? Better tools, better data, better coordination, anything.

Better tools would help a lot.

## 9. What are we missing? What should we be looking at that we haven't asked about?

> Anything that didn't fit the questions above. Operational pain points, data quality issues, blind spots, ideas, concerns, side topics — whatever you think we should know.

Better communication with MRC. Sometimes the trucks show up late and it makes it to where we ran out of space.
