# ADR-0019.1: Bi-weekly pay-period cadence and signature timing

**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Status:** Accepted
**Extends:** ADR-0019 (Bonus Management System)

> **Post-acceptance note (2026-06-09):** the §6 **tier-4** "payroll deadline missed"
> alert was originally implemented as `state != 'paid'`. After ADR-0023 added the
> `historical_imported` state, that query began matching archival periods and
> false-fired on the Period-13 go-live. **ADR-0025** corrects tier-4 to an allowlist
> of live pre-`paid` states and hardens ntfy delivery with retries. Read §6 together
> with ADR-0025.

## Context

ADR-0019 originally specified the Bonus Management System on a **monthly** reporting cadence. After implementation but before any production data shipped, Bill verified against the SVdP 2026 Payroll Calendar and identified the cadence as **bi-weekly**, not monthly. The calendar's footer phrase "bi-monthly, meaning twice per month" refers specifically to **benefit deductions** — not pay periods. Pay periods are bi-weekly (26/year, 14 days fixed, Tue → Mon, Friday pay-date).

The original monthly schema, state machine, close cron, and signature timing must be revised before production data accrues. Zero production bonus rows exist (M365 mail-send still dark per T-122), so this is a pre-data correction — no migration of historical bonus records.

Additionally, Bill set a **hard payroll deadline**: PDF must be in `payroll@svdp.us` no later than **9:00 AM PT on the Tuesday of pay week**. This deadline is non-negotiable and gates the entire signature workflow design.

## Decision

### 1. Bi-weekly period structure

Pay periods are **14 days fixed**, Tuesday → Monday, with Friday pay-date.

- 26 periods per year
- Anchored on SVdP's 2026 Payroll Calendar
- Periods drift across calendar months (e.g., Period 12 of 2026 spans 2026-05-26 → 2026-06-08, crossing the May/June boundary)
- Pay date is the Friday of the same calendar week as the closing Monday (i.e., Mon period-end + 4 days = Fri pay-date)
- "Holiday-adjusted" pay dates (the asterisked Fridays on the SVdP calendar) are payroll-internal handling for **deposit date shifts**. DR3-Vision uses the canonical Friday for `pay_date` and is not concerned with the actual deposit date — only with delivering the PDF by 9:00 AM PT Tuesday of pay week, which is unaffected by the deposit shift.
- The "3-paydays-per-month skip benefit deductions" rule in the calendar footer is payroll-internal and not modeled in DR3-Vision.

### 2. Hard payroll deadline: Tuesday 9:00 AM Pacific

The signed bonus PDF must be in `payroll@svdp.us` no later than **9:00 AM PT on the Tuesday of pay week**. This is the same Tuesday on which the period that just ended starts being "the prior period." The window from period-end Mon to deadline is **~15.5 hours** — most of which is overnight.

The tightness of this window dictates the close-and-sign timeline (§3 below) and forces the **auto-override** mechanism (§4) so a single absent signer cannot block payroll.

### 4. Auto-override mechanism

The auto-override is the load-bearing mechanism for hitting the 9:00 AM deadline reliably.

**Trigger:** Tuesday 08:30 AM PT escalation cron tick, when any signature slot on a `pending_signatures` or `partially_signed` period is still unsigned.

**Action:** the system signs the missing slot(s) **as Bill** (`actor_user_id` = Bill's user UUID). This is the same code path as a manual admin override, called with `actor_user_id = bill`, `override_reason = "system-applied admin override per ADR-0019.1 escalation policy after [slot(s)] failed to sign by 08:30 AM PT deadline"`, and a new `*_auto_override_at` timestamp column set to the cron tick time.

**Audit:** every auto-override writes:

- An `audit_log` row with `actor_label = 'system:bonus-escalation'`, `actor_user_id = bill`, `action = update`, `table_name = bonus_pay_periods`, full before/after JSON
- An ntfy publish to `dr3-vision-system` with fingerprint `bonus-auto-override:<site>:<period>` confirming the action and the slot(s) overridden

**PDF attestation language:** the signature block on the PDF clearly distinguishes a system override from a human override:

> _Signed by Bill Barnard, Administrator, on behalf of Janette Thomas, Facility Manager._
> _System-applied admin override per ADR-0019.1 escalation policy. Janette Thomas did not sign by 08:30 AM PT on Tue Jun 9, 2026._

This is structurally identical to a manual admin override (per ADR-0019 §5) but with explicit language flagging the automated origin. Payroll sees clearly that the slot was not manually signed.

**Critical operational dependency:** Bill's Entra account MUST stay active and unlocked for the auto-override to fire correctly. Two safeguards:

- The escalation cron's 07:30 AM PT urgent ntfy goes to ALL override-authorized humans (Bill, plus Morena for Woodland and Kelsey for Eugene). If Bill is unreachable, another human can manually sign with override before 08:30 fires.
- The Vision Dashboard health pill subsystem panel surfaces "override actor available" (boolean: can the system locate Bill's user record and is it active). A red state here means the auto-override path is broken and Bill needs to investigate.

### 6. Cron infrastructure changes

**Replace `scripts/bonus-month-close.mjs`** with `scripts/bonus-period-close.mjs`. The new script runs daily at 17:30 PT (via container UTC schedule with Pacific-aware date check using `@/lib/time`), looks up `bonus_pay_periods WHERE period_end = appToday() AND state = 'draft'`, and transitions any matches to `pending_signatures` via the state machine. Single-call path; idempotent (state machine rejects double-transition).

**New `scripts/bonus-escalation-check.mjs`** runs three times every Tuesday (cron schedule expressed in Pacific via `@/lib/time` checks):

- 06:00 PT — low-urgency ntfy if any slot on yesterday's-closed period is still unsigned. ntfy topic `dr3-vision-system`, fingerprint `bonus-escalation-warning:<site>:<period>:t1`, default priority 3.
- 07:30 PT — urgent ntfy. Same fingerprint base, `:t2` suffix, priority 4. Includes the full list of override-authorized humans in the message body.
- 08:30 PT — auto-override execution. For each unsigned slot, call the existing signature service with `actor_user_id = bill`, `override_reason = ADR-0019.1 escalation message`, and the new `*_auto_override_at` timestamp. Triggers PDF generation + M365 mail-send via the existing post-signature side-effect chain.

**`scripts/bonus-eod-check.mjs`** stays as-is. It already runs daily at 17:00 PT and is site-scoped; it fires for any active workday (not just period-end Mondays). The bi-weekly cadence is orthogonal to daily EOD enforcement.

### 7. URL paths — `/bonus/months/*` unchanged

The Next.js route group at `/bonus/months/*` keeps its path. Renaming to `/bonus/periods/*` or `/bonus/pay-periods/*` would invalidate bookmarks (Bill, Janette, Rick all have direct links). Internally the route handlers, page components, and data layer use period-named functions and types; the URL segment is just a stable label.

Optional follow-up (V2.1 polish): add a redirect from `/bonus/periods/*` to `/bonus/months/*` so the more semantically accurate path also works.

## Bootstrapping question — Period 12 of 2026

If go-live is **Mon Jun 8, 2026**, the first close cron fires that same evening at 17:30 PT against **Period 12 of 2026** (Tue May 26 → Mon Jun 8). Period 12 has no production daily entries because the bonus surface was only shipped to production on 2026-06-06 (Saturday) and no entries have been keyed.

Three options, decided by Bill:

1. **Skip Period 12.** Manually transition Period 12 to a terminal `skipped` state (new enum value) at deploy time. First "real" payroll PDF is Period 13 (Tue Jun 9 → Mon Jun 22, pay date Fri Jun 26).
2. **Backdate Period 12.** Bill uses the admin-only `AdminDatePicker` capability (shipped today 2026-06-06) to fill in Janette's recollection of daily counts for May 26 – Jun 8. Period 12 closes Mon Jun 8 17:30 PT with the recollected data.
3. **Manual partial Period 12.** Janette enters whatever fragments she has; system flags partial in the PDF.

Default if Bill doesn't specify: **Option 1 (skip Period 12)**. Safest — no fabricated data, no manual reconstruction, clean break at the cadence-change boundary. Period 13 is the first canonical bi-weekly payroll PDF.

## Alternatives considered

- **Cron-based close (every other Monday)** — Rejected. Cron can't express "every other Monday" cleanly. The daily-tick + Pacific-aware date check is simpler and more robust to DST.
- **Application-level scheduler instead of cron** — Considered. The fleet runs cron containers as a standard pattern; deviating would add operational complexity for no benefit.
- **Tuesday 8:00 AM auto-override (longer buffer to deadline)** — Originally proposed; rejected by Bill in favor of 08:30 AM. The 30-minute buffer is sufficient given known M365 retry-chain timing; the extra 30 minutes of human signing window is worth more.
- **Tier 4 ntfy at 09:00 if deadline missed despite auto-override** — Yes, added. If somehow the auto-override fires but mail-send fails past 09:00 (M365 outage, R2 outage, etc.), ntfy `bonus-payroll-deadline-missed:<site>:<period>` fires at urgent. Bill manually intervenes.
- **Pay date as anchor instead of period_end as anchor** — Rejected. Period ends are when the workflow starts; pay dates are when the money lands. The close-and-sign workflow keys off period_end. Pay date is metadata for PDF titling and reporting.
- **Keep `bonus_months` table name, only repurpose semantics** — Rejected. Misleading for future maintainers. Pre-data rename is cheap; do it cleanly.

## Consequences

- The bonus PDF title block reads "DR3 Woodland Bonus Report — Period 13: Jun 9–22, 2026 · Pay date Jun 26" instead of "DR3 Woodland Bonus Report — June 2026".
- Annual aggregate views still tile by calendar year (Jan 1 – Dec 31) for SVdP fiscal alignment. Some periods straddle the year boundary; Period 1 of 2026 includes 9 days from 2025 (Dec 23 – Dec 31). The aggregate counts those days under the calendar year they fall in (so December 2025 days are NOT in the 2026 aggregate), not under the pay-date year. Per-employee history pages show both views: by pay-period and by calendar year.
- Per-employee history pages display 26 rows per year instead of 12. Layout already accommodates this (table is scrollable).
- The audit log shows ~2x more bonus events per year. Indefinite retention unchanged.
- M365 send rate goes from ~12/year to ~26/year per site = 52/year tenant-wide. Still orders of magnitude under Graph's 10,000/10min limit.
- Bill becomes a critical operational dependency for the 08:30 PT auto-override. If Bill's account is locked or his user record is deactivated, the auto-override fails. Health-pill panel surfaces this state.
- Future Eugene enablement (per ADR-0019.2) inherits this cadence with no additional schema work.

## Amendment — payroll-day 07:00 close (2026-07-07, operator-directed)

**Change.** The period close moves from **17:30 PT on `period_end`** to **07:00
PT on the payroll day — the day AFTER `period_end`**. The signature/escalation
window shifts to the same morning, tightening the loop against the 09:00 PT
payroll deadline (T-206) while giving managers the morning of payroll day to
sign rather than requiring an evening-before action.

**What changed.**

- `scripts/bonus-period-close.mjs`: fire time `17:30` → `07:00`
  (`FIRE_HOUR_PT`/`FIRE_MINUTE_PT`; helper renamed `msUntilNext1730Pacific` →
  `msUntilNext0700Pacific`). Still a daily Pacific-aware tick (DST-robust); still
  a clean no-op on non-payroll days.
- Close route (`/api/internal/bonus/close-months`): predicate operand moved from
  `appToday()` to `previousDayKey(appToday())` — it now transitions every `draft`
  period whose `period_end` was **yesterday** (Pacific). The state-machine
  filter (`state = 'draft'`) is unchanged, so idempotency + re-fire safety hold.
- Escalation (`scripts/bonus-escalation-check.mjs`): tier **t1 06:00 -> 07:10**
  (06:00 now predates the close; t1 becomes a post-close nudge). t2 07:30 / t3
  08:30 (auto-override) / t4 09:00 (deadline) unchanged. The escalation route
  already scoped to "yesterday's-close" periods (`appToday()` minus 1 day), so it
  needed no logic change -- only its comment references were updated. The close
  and t1 now fire the same morning (07:00 then 07:10), aligned by construction.

**Why the route, not the state machine.** `closePayPeriodsDueForSignature(db,
now)` stays a generic "close all `draft` periods with `period_end == now`"; the
"day after" policy lives at the scheduling/route layer next to the timing change.

**Tested.** Pacific date matrix + DST spring-forward boundary for the 07:00 fire;
the escalation tier matrix at the new times; a route test asserting the close
targets `previousDayKey(appToday())`. Operator note: **P14 (both sites) sits in
`draft` by design tonight and closes at 07:00 PT the next payroll morning via
this new logic.**

### b. "Override actor available" health pill was never implemented

§4 of this ADR states:

> The Vision Dashboard health pill subsystem panel surfaces "override actor
> available" (boolean: can the system locate Bill's user record and is it
> active). A red state here means the auto-override path is broken.

**This does not exist.** A health pill shipped (`src/app/_components/health-pill.tsx`,
backed by `src/app/api/health/subsystems/route.ts`), but its subsystem list is
hardcoded and closed at six entries — `db`, `r2`, `mymrc`, `ntfy`, `graph`,
`glitchtip`. None reads `bonus_signature_chains`, and nothing outside
`src/lib/bonus/escalation.ts` checks the override actor's `is_active` at all.

The consequence is not theoretical. The `actorUnavailable` guard in
`escalation.ts` is a _detector at the point of failure_, not a standing
indicator: the only signal that the auto-override actor is dead arrives as an
urgent ntfy at 08:30 PT — thirty minutes before the payroll deadline, at the
moment the safety net has already declined to fire. Both sites sat in exactly
that state from the 2026-07-07 incident until 2026-08-11 with no visible symptom
(see ADR-0019.3). Building the pill remains the right fix and is **not** done
here; this amendment only stops the ADR from claiming a control that does not
exist.
