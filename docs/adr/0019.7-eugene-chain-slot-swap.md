## Separation of duties

ADR-0023 kept Patrick out of every chain slot because he is a Eugene `BonusEmployee`. ADR-0019.3 reversed that
for the ops slot. **This ADR reverses it for the facility slot, on Bill's explicit acceptance.**

Bill did not ask for the ADR-0019.3 §2 guard to be removed, so it stays exactly as built. It is keyed on (person,
period), not on a slot. Patrick may not sign any pay period that **contains his own bonus entries**, in any slot,
by any path. Facts that follow:

- For Period 21, Period 22 and every current or future period, the guard does not fire, because he has no entries in
  them. He signs facility normally. Tested: `eugene-chain-swap.test.ts`, "Patrick signs the facility slot,
  although he is a Eugene BonusEmployee".
- If one of the 27 historical periods that hold his entries is amended and re-signed, the guard refuses him with
  `sod_excluded` on facility. The period routes to the facility override chain (Rick or Bill), so it is never
  left unsignable. Tested.
- If Patrick is reactivated as a processor and keys bonus days, the periods holding those days are routed to the
  override chain in the same way. Letting him sign a period that pays himself would mean removing the guard. That
  is a separate decision for Bill, and this ADR does not take it.

## How it is applied

Migration `20260868_adr0019_7_eugene_chain_slot_swap`, one `DO` block, ships through the normal deployer path:

- It resolves users by email and site by code, then updates the Eugene row **only if it is in the exact
  pre-state above**. If the row is already swapped it is a no-op. In any other state it skips with a NOTICE and
  overwrites nothing. That is the 2026-08-11 lesson from ADR-0019.3: a swap phrased in terms of who holds a seat
  cannot see a newer occupant.
- It writes one `audit_log` row with before/after JSON and actor label `system:approver-swap (Bill instruction
2026-10-07 18:31 PDT, option "a": …; ADR-0019.7)`, the same label shape as the 2026-08-08 and 2026-08-11 swaps.
- On a clean replay (ADR-0035) there are no users, so it is a no-op.

The 2026-08-11 swap was a hand-applied data fix plus a seed fix. This one is a migration, because a migration is
reviewed, replayed in CI against a real Postgres, and applied by the deployer without anyone touching prod rows.
`prisma/seed/bonus_signature_chains.csv` carries the same row, so a re-seed reinforces the change (ADR-0019.3 §3).
The `users.csv` notes for Rick and Patrick are updated.

No access change was needed. `checkBonusAccess` is role + site, and both are Eugene `manager`s. Slot authority,
override authority, amendment approvers (symmetric facility↔ops) and chain health all read the chain row. The
chain cache (`getSignatureChain`, TTL) is per process, and the deploy recreates the app and cron containers.
