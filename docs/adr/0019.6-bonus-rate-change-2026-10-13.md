# ADR-0019.6: Bonus rate change from 2026-10-13, both sites, priced by entry date

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Status:** Accepted
**Approved by:** Bill Barnard, 2026-10-07 17:12 PDT
**Amends:** ADR-0019 §1 (the rule parameters are data, the formula is unchanged), ADR-0019.2 (Eugene's parameters)
**Supersedes for pricing:** the "rule effective on the period's first day" selection used by every period-level read since ADR-0019

## Decision

From **Tuesday 2026-10-13**, Woodland (CA) and Eugene (OR) pay the same daily processor bonus:

```
daily_bonus = MAX(units - 60, 0) * $1.00 + MAX(units - 100, 0) * $0.25
```

`threshold_low = 60`, `rate_low = $1.00`, `threshold_high = 100`, `rate_high = $0.25`. Units 61–100 earn $1.00 each and
units 101+ earn $1.25 each. Units are paid units: `mattress_count + saves`, tiered once per day (ADR-0083).

| Units | 60  | 65    | 70     | 80     | 90     | 100    | 101    | 120    | 150     |
| ----- | --- | ----- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------- |
| Pay   | $0  | $5.00 | $10.00 | $20.00 | $30.00 | $40.00 | $41.25 | $65.00 | $102.50 |

The rules it replaces were read from production on 2026-10-07 (both `effective_date 2026-01-01`, no end date):

| Site     | threshold_low | rate_low | threshold_high | rate_high | 80 units |
| -------- | ------------- | -------- | -------------- | --------- | -------- |
| woodland | 50            | $0.50    | 74             | $0.25     | $16.50   |
| eugene   | 50            | $1.00    | 100            | $0.25     | $30.00   |

**The change applies only to days dated on or after 2026-10-13.** A day on or before 2026-10-12 is priced at the old
rule forever, including when its period is amended and re-signed after 10/13.

### Pay-period timing

Periods are bi-weekly, Tuesday to Monday. Period 21 (Sep 29 – Oct 12, paid Oct 16) is entirely old-rate. Period 22
(Oct 13 – Oct 26, paid Oct 30) is the first new-rate period. The boundary falls on a period start, but the
implementation does not rely on that: see below.

## How it is applied

1. **Migration** `20260867_adr0019_6_bonus_rate_change_20261013`, one `DO` block: for each site, set
   `end_date = 2026-10-12` on the open rule that started before 10/13, and insert the new rule with
   `effective_date = 2026-10-13`, `end_date = NULL`. Rates on the historical rows are not edited. The close only
   matches an open rule and the insert is skipped when a 10/13 row exists, so a re-run is a no-op. The block ends by
   raising if any two rows for one site have overlapping windows, which rolls the whole change back. On a clean
   replay (ADR-0035) there are no sites, so both statements are no-ops and the seed supplies the rows.
2. **Seed CSV** `prisma/seed/processor_bonus_rules.csv` carries the same four rows (old rows windowed, new rows
   open), and the seed's exact-count assertion moves from 2 to 4.
3. **No switch on the day.** The rows are effective-dated, so the deploy can land any time before 10/13. Nothing
   reads the wall clock to choose a rate. Every day before 10/13 still resolves to the old row after the
   migration (proved by `processor-bonus-rate-change.db.test.ts` against a real Postgres).

## Why pricing moved to the entry date

Before this ADR, the rule was chosen as follows (origin/main at `5f0b4f3`):

- **Per period, by `period_start`** — the sign-time payout lock (`signatures.ts:460-468`), the ADR-0033 reconcile
  recompute (`reconcile-fetch.ts:76`), the period list (`month-list.ts:172`), the period page and amendment editor
  (`app/bonus/months/[id]/page.tsx:139`), the payroll PDF (`app/internal/bonus-pdf/[month-id]/page.tsx:155-156`),
  live standings (`current-period.ts:178, 257`), and the employee history and annual CSV export
  (`aggregates.ts:122-137`, cached by the period start's year-month).
- **Per day, by the entry's own date** — the daily entry grid (`daily-entry.ts:209`) and the 8 PM daily report
  (`daily-report.ts:215`).
- **By "today"** — nowhere. No path priced an entry by the current date, so amending a September entry on
  October 20 would already have used the Sep 29 period start, and the old rate.

`resolveActiveRule` honoured `end_date` (`end_date IS NULL OR end_date >= day`) but did not detect overlap: it took
the latest `effective_date` and ignored any other covering row.

For 10/13 the period-start selection gives the right answer, because the boundary is a period start. It would
misprice any change that lands inside a period, pricing the earlier days at the new rate, and nothing would flag
it. Payroll should not depend on a calendar coincidence, so every pay read now prices **each entry by the rule
covering its own `entry_date`**:

- `src/lib/bonus/rule-book.ts` (pure, also used by the client amendment editor): `ruleForDate` returns the single
  row whose window contains the day. It throws `OverlappingBonusRulesError` when two rows cover the day and
  `NoActiveRuleError` when none does. `historicalFallback` keeps the ADR-0023 behaviour of falling back to the
  site's earliest rule for imported periods that predate the rule table.
- `daily-entry.ts`: `loadRuleBook` reads every row for the site once (a handful of rows). `loadRuleLookup` and
  `loadHistoricalRuleLookup` bind it. `resolveActiveRule` and `resolveRuleForHistorical` are now thin wrappers, so
  the single-date paths get overlap detection too.
- `paid-units.ts`: `periodBonusCentsFor(rows, ruleFor)` takes a lookup and requires `entry_date` on every row, so
  a caller that forgets to select it gets a compile error instead of a period priced at one date's rate.
- Every consumer listed above now prices by `ruleFor(entry.entry_date)`. The lock, the reconcile, the PDF, the
  period page, the list, standings and the annual export all share `dailyBonusCentsFor` through the same lookup,
  so the figures on screen, on the signed PDF and in the CSV still cannot diverge.
- The standings banner's "qualifies above N units/day" uses the rule for today, clamped into the open period. It
  is display only; the money is always priced per entry date.

A static guard (`rate-change-20261013.test.ts`) fails if any source file calls `resolveActiveRule` or
`resolveRuleForHistorical` with a period start. Against origin/main it matches 6 files; against this change it
matches 0.

## Consumers audited for duplicated rates or thresholds

- **UI** (daily grid, amendment editor, standings, employee page): no literal rates. The threshold text comes from
  the rule.
- **i18n** (`src/i18n/locales/{en,es,ur}`): no bonus rate or threshold strings.
- **Signed PDF, CSV/annual export, aggregates, reconcile tripwire**: all priced through the lookup. Tests cover
  lock = PDF grand total for a Period 22 sample.
- **Processor quota (ADR-0071)**: the quota is 75 production units, read from `processor_quota_config`. It is a
  production bar, not derived from the bonus threshold, and is unaffected. 75 is still above the new 60 floor.
- **Escalation, chain health, EOD check, daily-report email**: no rate literals.
- **Comments and docs** that stated the old Woodland rule as current (`calculator.ts` header,
  `prisma/seed/README.md`, `PROJECT-CHARTER.md` §bonus) were updated or annotated. Historical CHANGELOG entries
  and older ADRs stay as written.

## Out of scope

- The Eugene signature chain and signers (`bonus_signature_chains.csv`, users, access matrix). The rate change
  does not depend on who signs.
- **Pre-existing, not changed here:** the 8 PM daily report's per-line bonus figure is computed on processed units
  only (`daily-report.ts`, ADR-0083 production-quantity reading). That figure is informational, not payroll. On a
  day with saves it is lower than what the period will lock. It is recorded in `docs/OPEN-ITEMS.md` for Bill to
  decide.
- The comparison document `dr3-bonus-compare-ca-or-20261006` (2026-10-06) is point-in-time. It describes the
  pre-10/13 rules and is superseded by this ADR for anything dated from 2026-10-13.

## Verification

- `src/lib/bonus/__tests__/rate-change-20261013.test.ts` checks the approved payout table, that each site's seed
  book covers every day 2026-01-01 → 2027-12-31 exactly once, and the old rule on 10/12 against the new rule on
  10/13 at 80 units. It also covers Period 21 re-pricing (old rate), a synthetic straddling period (each day by its
  own rule), Period 22 at the new rate, lock = PDF for Period 22, the overlap and end-date guards, migration SQL
  matching the CSV, and the static guard.
- `src/lib/bonus/processor-bonus-rate-change.db.test.ts` (ADR-0078 real-DB lane) runs the migration twice against
  a production-shaped Postgres. It checks two rows per site with the old rates untouched, then the real resolver on
  10/12 and 10/13 and on deploy day (10/07). It also checks that an overlap aborts and rolls back.
